Last week's method has been tested again, and I am pleased to report that the literature now contains two data points.

## Replication

Thursday, 10:15 p.m., same kitchen, same route. [He fell asleep on lap six](obs:observation-08). That is two attempts and two successes, and I have upgraded my position on [walking](claim:claim-walking) from tentative to strong. [Mom](thing:mom) has reviewed the finding and requested a sample size larger than two. I have explained that this is every sample there is.

## The forty minutes

On Saturday [the blue pacifier](thing:blue-pacifier) went missing. I would like to describe what followed calmly. [It was gone for forty minutes, during which he rejected the green pacifier four times](obs:observation-09), each time with the expression of a man handed the wrong coat.

I previously suggested the two pacifiers were the same. I withdraw the remark. [He will only accept the blue one](claim:claim-blue-only), and [the green pacifier](thing:green-pacifier) will from now on be referred to as the decoy. The blue one was under a sofa cushion. It has been promoted to the only acceptable pacifier and is no longer allowed to leave the room unaccompanied.

## A theory about the swaddle

Mom has a theory, and it is more complicated than mine, so it does not fit in one sentence: [the swaddle works, but only if it is applied before he realizes what is happening](claim:claim-swaddle). Once he has seen it coming, [the swaddle](thing:swaddle) is a puzzle, and he is good at puzzles. Tonight [he had one arm out in under four minutes](obs:observation-10), having watched the whole thing being folded.

I endorse the theory. I also endorse not being the one who has to apply it.

## The Schedule, briefly

[The Schedule](claim:claim-schedule) is being followed in spirit. [The Baby](thing:baby) observed one of its eleven listed events this week, which was a nap, at a time of his choosing.
