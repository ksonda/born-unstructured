This installment is mostly corrections. I am publishing them promptly, in the spirit of the scientific method and at the request of the other author.

## Walking

[The Baby](thing:baby) has rejected walking. On Sunday night [he screamed on lap one, with the dishwasher running](obs:observation-16). In the interest of full disclosure, the dishwasher was running empty, because we had already washed everything we own.

I therefore withdraw the claim that [walking helps him settle](claim:claim-walking). It helped him settle twice, in December, and I will always have that.

The narrower claim survives, narrowly. [Walking works while the dishwasher is running](claim:claim-walking-dishwasher), provided the dishwasher contains dishes. [Mom](thing:mom) says that a theory which needs a full load in order to hold is a theory about the dishwasher. She continues to dispute it.

## The dishwasher

On Tuesday [he slept for two hours next to the dishwasher on a normal cycle](obs:observation-17). Nobody walked. I am not ready to say that Mom is right. I am ready to say that [the dishwasher is](claim:claim-dishwasher), tentatively, and I have been asked to note that these are the same sentence.

## The Schedule

[The Schedule was last consulted on day nine](obs:observation-19). It is still on the fridge, where it now mainly records what we hoped. I withdraw the claim that [he would be following it by week six](claim:claim-schedule). He is in week six. He follows nothing. [The Schedule](thing:the-schedule) will stay on display as a document of largely historical interest.

## Two smaller notices

[He refused the blue pacifier for an entire afternoon, then took it at 6 p.m. as though nothing had happened](obs:observation-18). Mom has qualified [her claim](claim:claim-blue-only): he accepts only [the blue pacifier](thing:blue-pacifier), subject to exceptions he declines to explain.

And on Thursday [he watched the swaddle being unfolded and began escaping before it was on](obs:observation-20). [The swaddle theory](claim:claim-swaddle) says it works if he does not realize what is happening. He now realizes what is happening. The theory stands, with a note that its one condition can no longer be met.

That is everything we were wrong about this week. Mom would like it recorded that she has withdrawn nothing.
